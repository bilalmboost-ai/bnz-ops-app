BNZ Ops Suite Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

BNZ Ops Suite is a back-office app for general contractors in the United States.

What we collect

Company name, city, phone, email, job names, vendor names, and messages you type to agents. In version 1 this data stays on your device unless you later connect a company cloud account.

What we do not collect

Precise location, contacts, payment cards, or bonding figures. We do not sell data, run ads, or track you across other apps.

AI

Agent replies help draft RFQs and estimate checklists. They are not a licensed estimate, bid, or legal advice.
