## Description
BNZ Ops Suite is the back-office app built by BNZ Builders INC for general contractors in the United States.

Three agents work inside your company workspace:

- Sara — procurement and inbox. Drafts RFQs by trade and chases quotes so bids do not stall.
- Emma — subcontractor desk. Sources and tracks vendors per job.
- Taylor — master estimator. Builder-style takeoff checklists. He will not invent a bid number from a chat line.

Each company is a separate workspace.
